# Hatamoto trademark policy

The Hatamoto **code** is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can use,
copy, modify, host and sell it; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The Hatamoto **name and logo** are not covered by that licence. They are
trademarks of Zen Labs Corporation. This policy explains how you may use them.
Its purpose is simple: when people see "Hatamoto", they should be able to trust
that it means the official software from the Hatamoto project, or something
that honestly describes its relationship to it.

## Allowed without asking

- **Talking about Hatamoto.** Articles, tutorials, videos, books, courses,
  conference talks and reviews.
- **Saying what your site runs on.** "Powered by Hatamoto" or "Built with
  Hatamoto".
- **Hosting Hatamoto.** You may host Hatamoto for yourself or for customers,
  including as a paid service, and describe it accurately:
  "Hatamoto hosting", "Managed hosting for Hatamoto", "One-click Hatamoto
  install".
- **Agencies and freelancers.** "We build websites with Hatamoto", "Hatamoto
  development", "Hatamoto migration services".
- **Plugins and themes.** Name them "*Your Name* for Hatamoto" or
  "*Your Name*, a Hatamoto plugin".
- **Unmodified redistribution.** Sharing official, unmodified Hatamoto releases
  under the name Hatamoto.
- **Community groups and events** that are free or not-for-profit, such as a
  local "Hatamoto Meetup", provided they do not suggest they are run or
  endorsed by Zen Labs Corporation.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Implying an official relationship.** Words like "Official", "Certified",
  "Endorsed", "Partner" or "Hatamoto Cloud", or anything else suggesting your
  product or service is made, endorsed or supported by Zen Labs Corporation
  or the Hatamoto project.
- **Hatamoto in your company, product or service name.** For example
  "Hatamoto Hosting Ltd", "HatamotoHost" or "Hatamoto Pro". Describe what you
  do ("hosting for Hatamoto") instead of naming yourself after it.
- **Hatamoto in a domain name** or a social media handle, such as
  `hatamotohosting.com` or `@hatamoto_cloud`.
- **Plugins or themes that start with the mark**, for example
  "Hatamoto SEO" (use "SEO for Hatamoto").
- **Modified versions (forks) under the Hatamoto name.** You are free to fork
  the code. A fork that you distribute or offer as a service must use a
  different name and logo. You may say it is "based on Hatamoto".
- **Changing the logo** or combining it with your own marks.
- **Commercial merchandise** using the name or logo.

## Using the logo

You may use the unmodified Hatamoto logo to link to the Hatamoto project or to
show that a site or product works with Hatamoto, as long as it is not more
prominent than your own branding.

## Questions and permission

If you are not sure whether your use is allowed, or you would like permission
for a use not listed here, write to **trademarks@hatamoto.dev** before you
launch. Say what you plan to call it, where it will appear and who runs it.

## Brand assets

The official logo files and the rules for using them (clear space, colours,
minimum size) are on [hatamoto.dev](https://hatamoto.dev). Use those files
unmodified. Until the brand assets page is published there, ask at the address
above.

## Registration status

Zen Labs Corporation claims the Hatamoto name and logo as trademarks. The
status of any filed or registered marks, and the countries they cover, is to be
confirmed and will be listed here. This policy applies whether or not a mark
is registered in your country.

Zen Labs Corporation may update this policy. Uses that followed the policy at
the time they began will be treated fairly.
