The admin is the private part of your site, where you and your colleagues write, publish and organise everything visitors see. It runs on the same server as your site, works in any browser, on a phone and with JavaScript turned off.

These pages are for editors and site owners. The guides walk you through everyday jobs one step at a time: finding your way around, publishing a page or a post, building a menu, inviting a colleague. The reference pages list every screen, option and limit for when you need the detail.
