Search engines and AI tools read your site as well as people do. This section shows you what they get from it, how to tell AI crawlers what they may use, how to see which ones visit and how many visitors they send back, how to let an AI agent of your own work on your content, and how to keep your page addresses, sitemap and structured data right. All of it is built in and needs no plugin.

The guides are written for site owners and take you through one task each, in the admin. The reference pages are for developers and list the exact behaviour: every file Hatamoto generates, every setting, limit and error.
