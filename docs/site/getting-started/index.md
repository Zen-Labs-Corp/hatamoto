This section is for the person setting up a new Hatamoto site. You do not need to be a developer: if you can copy a command into a terminal and have used a hosting panel, you can follow it. At the end you have a running site, an owner account and accounts for the people who help you.

Once the site runs, the usual next jobs are to put it behind HTTPS with [Reverse proxy and TLS](../operate/reverse-proxy-tls.md) and [Hardening](../operate/hardening.md), to set its public address from the [Configuration reference](../operate/configuration-reference.md), to set up [Email](../operate/email.md) at **Settings > Email** if you skipped it in the wizard, and to plan [Backups](../operate/backups.md) and [updates](../updates/index.md). To change the look or add features, see [Themes](../themes/index.md) and [Plugins](../plugins/index.md). To bring an existing site with its old addresses, see [Move your site](../migrate-contribute/move-your-site.md).

## Start here

Follow these in order the first time:

1. [Before you install](before-you-install.md): check your server and gather what the install asks for.
2. [Install the binary](install-binary.md) for a server you will keep, or [Install with Docker](install-docker.md) to try Hatamoto on your own computer: either way your site starts and prints a setup code.
3. [First run](first-run.md): create your owner account, name your site and set up email.
4. [Create users](create-users.md): add the people who will work on the site, and reset a password.
5. [A tour of the admin](../admin/tour.md): find your way around the screens you will use.
6. [Edit and publish content](../admin/content.md): write your first page and put it online.
